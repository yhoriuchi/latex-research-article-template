\section{Results} 
\label{sec:results}

This sample results section shows where authors can present the principal estimates and evaluate the expectations. One sentence can describe the direction and magnitude of the central estimate. Another can report the relevant measure of uncertainty without repeating every value shown in a figure or table. Authors can then explain the substantive meaning of the result and distinguish it from statistical significance. A final sentence can summarize the most informative robustness or subgroup analysis. Replace these sample sentences with the article's results and substantive interpretation.
